A child-facing video app for learning, built from NouTube. A child sees only the videos a parent has approved.
<ul><li>Bengali-first: বাংলা শিখি, বাংলা পড়া, ছড়া, সংখ্যা ও গণিত</li><li>English learning: ABCD, phonics, rhymes, counting and vocabulary</li><li>General learning: shapes, colours, animals, fruits, everyday knowledge, good habits and safety</li><li>Islamic learning in Bengali: Quran, duas, the stories of the prophets, adab and akhlaq</li><li>Shorts, search, feeds and channel browsing are removed</li><li>Autoplay is off, and a video never rolls into the next one</li><li>External links are blocked</li><li>No ads in the app</li></ul>
<h2>How it works</h2>
<ul><li>An allowlist of approved videos is the only source of content: an unapproved video is refused</li><li>A parent approves, rejects, adds and removes videos and channels behind a PIN</li><li>There is no default PIN, and no video is approved by default</li></ul>
<h2>Please note</h2>
<ul><li>This is a client-side filter on top of YouTube, not a tamper-proof parental control. Use the device's own parental controls as well.</li><li>The bundled candidate list has not been reviewed; every item starts as pending for a parent to look at.</li></ul>
